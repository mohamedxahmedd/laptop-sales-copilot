- Exact-hardware-only requests open from the exact requested part and never claim a use case the customer did not state.

- Programming/study copy prioritizes CPU/RAM/SSD and explicitly treats GPU as secondary unless relevant.
- Engineering/3D, editing, Photoshop, and AI each have different benefit ordering and copy strategy.
- Named games/programs can be mentioned naturally, without making unverified performance guarantees.
- Removed repetitive phrases and generic Touch/SSD-first copy when they are not central.
